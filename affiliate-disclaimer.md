<div class="mainheading">
    <h1 class="sitetitle">Commercial Transparency & Affiliate Policy</h1>
    <p class="lead">
        Trust and editorial independence are the foundation of everything we publish at <strong>Zoo Near Me</strong>. This disclosure explains our commercial policies, how we fund our operations, and our promise to you as a reader.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="row">
    <div class="col-md-8 col-lg-8">
        <div class="article-post">
            <h2>1. Strict Editorial Independence</h2>
            <div class="alert alert-success" role="alert">
                <strong><i class="fas fa-check-circle"></i> No Paid Reviews or Sponsored Rankings:</strong> We never accept financial compensation, free entry tickets, or corporate perks from zoos, wildlife sanctuaries, or aquariums in exchange for positive reviews or favored placements in our comparison guides.
            </div>
            <p>Our comparative recommendations (such as determining the best zoo in a state for toddlers or visitors with disabilities) are based entirely on objective data: acreage, animal welfare accreditation, stroller and wheelchair accessibility, exhibit diversity, and transparent visitor pricing.</p>

            <hr>

            <h2>2. Affiliate Partnerships & How We Sustain Our Site</h2>
            <p>Operating an extensive, regularly audited directory requires significant ongoing research, data verification, and technical maintenance. To keep our content 100% free for all families and visitors, Zoo Near Me participates in selective affiliate marketing programs:</p>
            <ul>
                <li><strong>Amazon Services LLC Associates Program</strong>: Zoo Near Me is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com. If you click on an affiliate link for recommended travel gear (e.g., family backpacks, sensory headphones, or all-terrain stroller accessories) and make a purchase, we may earn a small referral commission at absolutely no additional cost to you.</li>
                <li><strong>Travel & Ticketing Partners</strong>: From time to time, we may link to authorized third-party ticketing platforms or hotel accommodation services. We only partner with established, reputable providers.</li>
            </ul>

            <hr>

            <h2>3. Full Search Engine & Regulatory Compliance</h2>
            <p>In accordance with the <strong>Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials</strong> and Google Search Central best practices:</p>
            <ul>
                <li>Every commercial or outbound monetization link on Zoo Near Me is tagged with <code>rel="sponsored nofollow"</code> to maintain complete transparency with search engine crawlers.</li>
                <li>Affiliate compensation never dictates our editorial opinions, ratings, or comparative conclusions. If a zoo has poor wheelchair accessibility or steep gravel paths, we state it plainly regardless of any commercial considerations.</li>
            </ul>

            <hr>

            <h2>4. Questions or Inquiries?</h2>
            <p>If you have questions regarding our commercial practices, affiliate relationships, or editorial governance, please reach out to our editorial team directly at <a href="mailto:contact@zoonear.me">contact@zoonear.me</a> or visit our <a href="/contact">Contact Page</a>.</p>

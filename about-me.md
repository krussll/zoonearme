        <h2>Connecting Families to America's Zoological Treasures</h2>
        <p class="lead">Hello, fellow wildlife lovers and family travelers!</p>
        <p>I'm Reece Williams, the founder and lead editor of <strong>Zoo Near Me</strong>. My fascination with the animal kingdom started in early childhood and evolved into a lifelong journey exploring America's public zoos, wildlife sanctuaries, and conservation centers from coast to coast.</p>
        <p>Over the past decade, I've journeyed through more than 30 U.S. states—from the mountainous wildlife habitats of Montana and the Upper Peninsula of Michigan to the historic zoological gardens of Missouri and Minnesota. Along the way, I discovered a major problem facing visitors: travel websites and official portals often bury critical logistical details like real-world terrain difficulty, gate price surprises, stroller navigation, and service animal guidelines.</p>

        <h3>Our Core Mission: Barrier-Free Family & Accessible Travel</h3>
        <p>I started Zoo Near Me on a mission to ensure that <strong>everyone</strong>—especially families with energetic toddlers, wheelchair users, neurodivergent children, and handlers with service dogs—can explore zoological parks with confidence and ease.</p>

        <div class="row mt-4 mb-4">

        <h3>Our Editorial Promise & Methodology</h3>
        <p>Unlike commercial travel portals that accept complimentary admissions or paid placements, <strong>Zoo Near Me maintains 100% editorial independence</strong>. We do not accept sponsored zoo rankings or paid reviews. Our comparisons are based on verified, objective criteria: ticket prices, acreage, dwell times, outside food allowances, and animal welfare standards set by the Association of Zoos & Aquariums (AZA).</p>
        <p>You can read the complete breakdown of how we gather data, audit ticket prices, and review facilities on our dedicated <a href="/review-methodology"><strong>Review Methodology & Editorial Standards</strong></a> page.</p>
